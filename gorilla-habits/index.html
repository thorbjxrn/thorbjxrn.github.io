Gorilla Habits

Your habits, one week at a time.

See the whole week in one row for each habit. Mark each day as Done, Missed or Skipped. Choose Skipped for a planned rest day to keep your streak and completion rate intact.

Download on the App Store

Up to five habits free on iPhone and iPad.

Room for a rest day

The soft greens and cream give the weekly grid a quiet feel. Say you went for a walk on Monday and Thursday and chose to rest on Wednesday. All three days have a place in the same row. By Sunday you can see which days worked for you.

You can settle into a routine with up to five habits free. If you want to look back further or make room for more habits, Premium is a one-time purchase. There’s no monthly subscription to keep track of.

Free and Premium

Free

Track up to five habits, even offline. Review this week and last week, explore your statistics and heatmap, or open the calendar to see the current month. The free version includes ads.

Premium

One purchase unlocks unlimited habits, your full saved history, weekly goals, additional themes, private iCloud sync and an ad-free experience. There’s no subscription.

Common questions

Can I check in from my Home Screen?

Yes. The free widget lets you mark one habit for today. Premium adds widgets for multiple habits, a single habit’s week and a heatmap.

Where are my habits stored?

Your habits are stored on your device. You can track them offline without an app account. Premium also supports private iCloud sync, which runs automatically when you’re signed in to iCloud and the service is available.

Which devices can I use?

Gorilla Habits runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later and iPad with iPadOS 17 or later.

Which accessibility features does Gorilla Habits support?

On iPhone and iPad, VoiceOver reads the habit, day and status in the weekly grid. Double-tap today or a past day to change its status. If you turn on Differentiate Without Color, symbols distinguish the daily marks. The app and its widgets support larger text, and the widgets also have VoiceOver labels.
