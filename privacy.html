Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Overview

This privacy policy covers all apps published by Thorbjørn Bonvik, including Stronq, Prāṇa, ChromoTune, Overdubber, Gorilla Habits (formerly Simple Habits), and Strophe, on the App Store and, for Overdubber, on Google Play. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what these apps access and how it is handled.

Data stored on your device

App data — such as workout history, breathing session records, habit tracking, and settings — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework (on Android, in the app's private storage). This data is never transmitted to any server we operate.

iCloud sync (where offered)

Gorilla Habits (formerly Simple Habits) syncs your habits through your own iCloud account after you buy the upgrade, and Strophe syncs your pieces whenever you are signed in to iCloud. This goes through Apple's iCloud service under your Apple Account; we cannot read it. The other apps keep their data on your device.

Apple Health (where relevant)

Some apps can read and/or write data to Apple Health with your permission — for example, logging bodyweight entries or completed workouts. Health data stays on your device and in iCloud (if you have Health syncing enabled). We never send Health data to any external service.

Microphone (where relevant)

Some apps use your device's microphone for real-time, on-device audio analysis — for example, pitch detection for tuning or capturing a recording you initiate. Audio is processed on your device; it is never uploaded, saved without your action, or transmitted anywhere by us.

Camera and photo library (where relevant)

Some apps can use your camera for a recording you start — for example, a performance video in Overdubber's Session — and can save the videos you choose to your photo library. The app never reads the rest of your library, and we never upload your recordings. A video you share or save is then handled by the app or service you send it to.

Motion sensors (where relevant)

Some apps read your device's motion to animate the screen — for example, Overdubber's holiday snowfall moves when you tilt your device. This stays on your device.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, or by Google Play for Overdubber on Android. We do not process or store any payment information.

Advertising

The free versions of Stronq, ChromoTune, Gorilla Habits (formerly Simple Habits) and Overdubber on iPhone and iPad show ads served by Google AdMob. All ads are non-personalized — these apps never use App Tracking Transparency and do not ask for permission to track you. AdMob may still collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure non-personalized ads. You can learn more in Google's Privacy Policy. The paid upgrade removes the ads. Prāṇa includes Google's ads library but does not currently show ads. The Mac-specific version of Overdubber, including its TestFlight builds, shows no third-party ads and includes no advertising library. The iPad version running on a Mac follows the iPad advertising practices above. Overdubber on Android shows no ads and includes no advertising library.

Analytics and tracking

Overdubber and Prāṇa send usage events to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service, so we can see which features are used and where people get stuck. An event says that something happened — for example, that a recording was made, an export finished, a breathing session ended and how long it lasted, or a purchase screen was shown or a purchase completed (without the price or the order number). Each event also carries the device details the analytics library adds, such as the device model, system version, language, region, time zone and app version.

Events never contain your recordings, the names of your projects or files, an account, or an advertising identifier. So that we can count how many people use an app, each event carries an identifier for your device that is turned into a one-way code before it leaves the device: on iPhone, iPad and Mac it is derived from the identifier Apple gives this developer's apps on your device, when available; on Android the app creates a random identifier when it is installed. We cannot turn that code back into your name or contact details. Events travel over an encrypted connection, and TelemetryDeck processes them on our behalf under its privacy policy. We do not sell them.

Stronq, ChromoTune, Gorilla Habits (formerly Simple Habits) and Strophe include no analytics. None of the apps ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Notifications

If an app offers reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No push notification service or external server is involved.

Children's Privacy

These apps are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

On iPhone, iPad and Android, deleting an app normally removes its local app data. On Mac, deleting the app does not necessarily remove its saved projects and settings. Delete unwanted projects and saved Sessions in Overdubber before uninstalling, and remove exported copies separately. Some app files and settings may remain; contact us if you want help removing them. A copy in your iCloud account stays there until you delete it in iCloud. On Android, Overdubber's Google account backup holds its settings only, never your recordings; moving the app to a new phone with Android's device transfer also copies your projects.

Usage events already sent to TelemetryDeck stay with TelemetryDeck, which does not set a fixed deletion date. Because events carry no name, we cannot find and erase one person's events; uninstalling an app stops new ones. Data Google keeps from serving ads is covered by Google's privacy policy. For any question about your data, write to the address below.

Changes to This Policy
