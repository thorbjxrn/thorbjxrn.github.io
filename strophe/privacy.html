Strophe — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Overview

Strophe is an app for memorizing lyrics, poetry, and speeches, built by Thorbjørn Bonvik. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

Data stored on your device

Pieces you add, practice progress, review history, and preferences (such as reminder hour) are stored on your device using Apple’s SwiftData framework and local settings. This app does not run its own backend for that content.

iCloud

If you are signed into iCloud and sync is available, your pieces and progress may sync across your devices through Apple’s CloudKit using your personal iCloud account. That data is under your Apple ID; the developer does not operate a separate content server or access your texts.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, practice reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Strophe does not use a third-party push provider for those reminders.

Advertising and tracking

Strophe does not include ads, analytics SDKs, or tracking frameworks. No personal data is collected, shared, or sold to third parties by the developer for advertising or analytics.

Purchases

There are no in-app purchases in the current version. If that changes, payment would be handled by Apple through the App Store; Strophe would not process payment card details.
